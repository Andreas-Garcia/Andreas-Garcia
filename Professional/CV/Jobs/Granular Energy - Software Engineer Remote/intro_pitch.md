# Self-introduction — 30-min screening call

I'm a backend engineer with over 10 years of professional experience, mostly focused on Python, REST APIs, and PostgreSQL-backed domain systems.

For the last few years I've been founder and lead developer of TheMusicTree, where I've built REST APIs with Django REST Framework and PostgreSQL — TheMusicTreeAPI and HearTheMusicTree — and designed the domain models and algorithms behind GrowTheMusicTree. I also published an open-source Python library, AudioMeta Python, with over 1,500 tests before releasing it on PyPI, where it's had around 26,000 downloads. Testing and clean domain modelling are things I care about a lot, not just as good practice but because they're what let you move fast without breaking things later.

Before that, I worked as a research engineer at INRAE, contributing to an open-source, FAIR research data platform supporting an agrivoltaics project — combining agriculture and photovoltaics — where I built REST API services and worked on fairly complex domain modelling with a SCRUM team.

I've also done some consulting work modelling and structuring a large, messy dataset — classifying over 1,300 raw categories into a clean taxonomy — which was a good exercise in the kind of analytical, structured thinking I imagine matters a lot for the matching and optimisation problem at the heart of your platform.

What drew me to Granular specifically is the combination of a Python/Postgres stack close to what I already do daily, and a mission — clean energy matching — that's genuinely aligned with what I care about, given my background around agrivoltaics and sustainability.
