They feed GrowTheMusicTree (https://grow.themusictree.org), a D3.js genre map backed by 30.9M songs in PostgreSQL. I run the whole stack myself, from Ansible provisioning to monitoring, and analyse the resulting genre graph in Jupyter notebooks.
