Description de la mission
About the role
Apply through our application form.

We are hiring a Forward Deployed Engineer to apply our models to each client project, adapting each time to the reality on the ground.

This role sits at the intersection of data science, engineering, and customer delivery operations. You will work closely with operations, engineering, product, and R&D to deploy and adapt CarbonFarm’s technical capabilities. That includes everything from running data pipelines and validating model outputs to shaping requirements and helping identify what should become part of the core product.

On the client-facing front, you will be working closely with our Customer Success Managers. They will manage client expectations. You will help translate client needs into technical delivery requirements and intervene in client communication when things get too technical.

This is not a consulting role. We are looking for someone who is comfortable working directly with data, code, and technical systems, and who can bridge the communication gap between a technical product and the real world in which our clients operate.

What you’ll do
Adapt pre-built models and data processing to the idiosyncrasies of each client project
Collaborate with the Customer Success Manager to deliver technical results to commercial partners
Run and maintain customer mission workflows from data intake to final output
Work with Python, SQL, cloud systems and machine-learning pipelines
Validate model outputs and investigate issues in data or execution
Support reporting and communicating the environmental outcomes for multiple customers in parallel
Translate customer needs into actionable technical requirements
Identify recurring delivery patterns that should be improved or productised
Contribute to product improvements over time as delivery work reveals missing capabilities
Leverage AI tooling to drive rapid iteration, with a strong bias for technical accuracy
